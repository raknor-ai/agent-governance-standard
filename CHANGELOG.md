# Changelog

## v1.1 (proposal window open)

**Comment period: 2026-09-23 to 2026-11-30**

The v1.1 proposal window is open for public comment against the published v1.0 standard. Proposals are submitted as labeled issues using the v1.1 Proposal template. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the process.

No v1.1 draft exists at this stage. Accepted proposals will be incorporated into a draft published for final review after the window closes.

## v1.0 (March 2026)

Initial release. 26 controls across 5 domains: Authority Governance, Observability, Interoperability, Safety & Reliability, and Adversarial Resilience. Compliance assessed through adversarial testing against live systems and verified through cryptographic evidence.
